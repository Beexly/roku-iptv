WHAT THIS IS
------------
A free, self-hosted streaming portal. Live TV, movies, and TV shows — no
subscription, no account, no credit card, no app to buy.

It works in ANY web browser on a phone, tablet, or laptop. Cast or mirror
from your phone to the Roku TV to watch on the big screen.

Just send them this one link:

  https://beexly.github.io/roku-iptv/portal.html

That's it. Everything else refreshes on its own.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO WATCH ON THE TV
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A — Cast from your phone (easiest):
  1. Open the link above on your phone.
  2. Pick a channel or movie.
  3. Use your phone's "Cast" / "Screen Mirroring" / "Smart View" to send it
     to the Roku.

Option B — Watch in a browser on the TV:
  If the Roku has a web browser, open the link directly on the TV.

Option C — Use the Roku IPTV app (backup):
  If you want a dedicated app on the Roku itself, install the free
  "Xtream IPTV Player" from the Roku Channel Store and add this playlist:

    https://beexly.github.io/roku-iptv/tv.m3u

  (The app can be buggy — the web portal in Option A is more reliable.)

WHAT'S INSIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Live TV: 1,000+ channels (News, Sports, Movies, Entertainment, Documentary,
  Kids, Music, Lifestyle, Religious, International, Local, Weather, Shopping)
- Movies & Series: 1,000+ titles (public-domain films and classic TV)
- TV Guide: program info for channels that have it
- Search: find channels or movies by name

The playlist rebuilds every day, so dead channels drop off and new ones appear
automatically. The link never changes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Nothing loads":
  Make sure you're on the portal link (portal.html), not the raw M3U URL.
  The M3U is for apps, not browsers.

"Video won't play on the Roku app":
  The Xtream IPTV Player app can be unreliable. Use the web portal and cast
  from your phone instead.

"Channel not found" or "404":
  Free streams go offline sometimes. The playlist is rebuilt daily, so dead
  channels are removed automatically.
